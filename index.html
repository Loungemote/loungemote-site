How it works

Ready in seconds.

No sign-in, no dongle, no pairing with a second remote. If your TV is on your Wi-Fi, you're most of the way there.

  1. {% include phone.html screen="choose-tv" alt="Choose your TV: the TVs found on this Wi-Fi network." %}
    1

    Open the app on your Wi-Fi

    Loungemote scans your network and lists the TVs and streaming devices it finds.

  2. {% include phone.html screen="pair" alt="Pair TV: the TV asks whether to trust this iPhone. Select Allow on the TV." %}
    2

    Approve it once on the TV

    Select Allow on the TV, or type the code it shows. You only do this the first time.

  3. {% include phone.html screen="remote" alt="The remote, connected to the Living Room TV." %}
    3

    Start pressing

    Your phone is now the remote. Next time, the app reconnects as soon as you open it.

Privacy

Private by design.

A remote has no reason to know who you are. Loungemote has no accounts and no servers, and your key presses go from your phone to your TV and nowhere else.

Read the privacy policy {% include icon.html name="arrow" %}
  • {% include icon.html name="useroff" %}

    No account

    No sign-up, no email address, nothing to log in to.

  • {% include icon.html name="eyeoff" %}

    No ads, no tracking

    The app contains no advertising or analytics code.

  • {% include icon.html name="wifi" %}

    Stays on your network

    Commands travel over your Wi-Fi, directly to the TV you selected.

  • {% include icon.html name="lock" %}

    Keys stay on your iPhone

    Pairing data is kept in the iOS Keychain and never sent to us.

One exception, stated plainly: when you cast to a Google Cast device, Google's Cast SDK sends Google diagnostic data that is not linked to you. It starts only when you use casting.
