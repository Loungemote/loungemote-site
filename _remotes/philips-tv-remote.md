## What you can do

Free:

- Find and pair your TV, then press a first key to check that it responds
- Demo TV to try every feature without a TV

With Loungemote Pro (eligible new subscribers can try the weekly plan free for 3 days):

- Directional pad, OK, Back and Home, volume, mute, channels and playback keys
- Turn the TV on over Wi-Fi, on TVs that support it
- Simple layout with big buttons
- Swipe touchpad for one-handed navigation
- Every TV at home: save several TVs and switch in a tap
- Casting: photos, slideshows, videos, files and links
- Siri and Shortcuts (iOS only, coming soon): turn the TV on or off or mute it by voice

Some features depend on the TV model and its software.

## Set up in three steps

1. Connect your phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks often block the connection.
2. Open Loungemote. It finds the TV on your network by itself.
3. Titan OS TVs show a code on the screen. Type it in the app. Saphi TVs need no pairing.

Pairing happens only the first time. Next time, the app reconnects when you open it. If the TV does not appear, choose **Add by IP address** and type the address from the TV's network settings.

## Turn the TV on from your phone

Many Philips TVs can start from the network. Look for a network standby or Wake-on-LAN setting in the TV's settings.

## Good to know

- On Titan OS and Saphi, Loungemote sends remote keys. The keyboard and the app launcher are not available.
- Philips TVs with Google TV or Android TV connect as [Google TV](../android-tv-remote/).
- Your phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Loungemote does not use infrared, and it cannot control the TV from outside your home.
