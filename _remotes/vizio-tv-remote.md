## What you can do

Free:

- Find and pair your TV, then press a first key to check that it responds
- Demo TV to try every feature without a TV

With Loungemote Pro (new subscribers can try it free for 3 days):

- Directional pad, OK, Back and Home, volume, mute, channels and playback keys
- Turn the TV on over Wi-Fi, on TVs that support it
- Switch HDMI inputs
- Simple layout with big buttons
- Apps in one tap: open the apps on the TV from your phone
- Swipe touchpad for one-handed navigation
- Every TV at home: save several TVs and switch in a tap
- Casting: photos, slideshows, videos, files and links
- Siri and Shortcuts: turn the TV on or off, mute it or open an app by voice

Some features depend on the TV model and its software.

## Set up in three steps

1. Connect your iPhone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks often block the connection.
2. Open Loungemote. It finds the TV on your network by itself.
3. The TV shows a code on the screen. Type it in the app.

Pairing happens only the first time. Next time, the app reconnects when you open it. If the TV does not appear, choose **Add by IP address** and type the address from the TV's network settings.

## Turn the TV on from your iPhone

Many Vizio TVs can start from the network. Look for a network standby or Wake-on-LAN setting in the TV's settings.

## Good to know

- Typing with the phone keyboard is not available on Vizio TVs.
- Your iPhone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Loungemote does not use infrared, and it cannot control the TV from outside your home.
